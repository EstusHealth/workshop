How the workshop scores

Day 1 has you work through a scorecard in the workbook to land on your current enterprise value. The logic underneath: value rises with more customers and more lifetime gross profit per customer, and falls with risk.

  • Six value adders: revenue, revenue growth, EBITDA, EBITDA margin, revenue retention, LTV:CAC.
  • Value detractors: key person, unicorn employee, key customer, single channel, market and data risk.
  • Ten masterpiece pieces: leadership team, marketing and delivery without the founder, multiple channels, recurring revenue, diverse customers, automated metrics, cash flow with growth, audit-ready books, $5M+ EBITDA.

LTV : CAC bands

LTV is lifetime gross profit, CAC is fully loaded. The ratio decides whether you push spend or pause and refine.

< 3:1Stop. Fix the offer.
3 to 5:1Healthy. Scale carefully.
5 to 10:1Spend more.
10:1+Spend hard.

One attendee's note: aim for about 8:1 when aggressively scaling a service business.

How this page scores

The official workbook isn't public, so this is a rebuild from attendee notes and Hormozi's own talks. Each input maps to 0 to 100. Your score blends adders (35%), risk (35%) and the ten pieces (30%).

The multiple runs 1x to 10x from that score, capped by size (small EBITDA rarely earns big multiples) and capped at 3x when the business can't run without you. Treat the dollar figure as a direction, not a valuation.
